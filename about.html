WHERE WE BEGAN

2018 — TODAY

A local idea,
with room to fly.

I Fly Air Solutions Ltd began in 2018 as iFly Travel, a travel agency led by founder and CEO Abdullahi Adan Hassan. As the business grew through relationships with major airlines and ticket sales, the team opened offices in Wajir, Eldoret, Lodwar, Malindi, Mombasa, Lamu and Eastleigh.

Seeing a need for better connections, the company became I Fly Air Solutions Ltd in December 2019 and received its Air Service License in 2020. Today, that same local spirit guides every journey.
