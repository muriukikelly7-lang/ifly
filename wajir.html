TRAVEL GUIDE

A place to
come together.

Wajir is one of the oldest towns in Kenya, in the country’s North Eastern region. Its name is said to come from a Borana word meaning “come together.” Pastoralist communities have shaped the area’s culture and hospitality.

The county has a hot, arid climate, with temperatures typically ranging from 21°C to 31°C. Pack for warm days and take time to discover its local history.
