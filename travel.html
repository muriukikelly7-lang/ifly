PACKING FOR YOUR FLIGHT

02

SAFETY COMES FIRST

Dangerous goods

Some items are prohibited in both checked and cabin baggage. Examples include explosives and fireworks, flammable or poisonous compressed gases, flammable liquids, poisons, radioactive or corrosive materials, and devices containing pyrotechnics.

Knives, scissors, tools, sporting equipment and some aerosols may not be permitted in the cabin. Local authorities may remove restricted items at the boarding gate. When in doubt, check with iFly before packing.

CHECK-IN & YOUR TICKET

Ticket policies

International check-inOpens 3 hours before departure; closes 1 hour before departure.

Domestic check-inOpens 1 hour 30 minutes before departure; closes 45 minutes before departure.

Ticket changes, amendments, validity and fare conditions depend on the ticket purchased. Review your booking terms or contact reservations for the rules that apply to your journey.

04

SPECIAL ASSISTANCE

Travel requirements

Some journeys need extra preparation, including travel with children, pregnancy, reduced mobility, or a medical condition. Contact iFly in advance so the team can confirm current requirements and arrange assistance.

For unaccompanied minors or special assistance, the reference site advises making arrangements at least 24 hours before departure. Medical equipment may require earlier approval. Requirements can differ by route and circumstance.

QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

How do I make a booking?

Contact the call centre on +254 736 989 645 or +254 735 773 684, email reservations@ifly.co.ke, or book through an authorised agent. The reference site lists M-Pesa and credit or debit card payments. Follow the booking instructions to complete payment.

How much baggage can I bring?

Your allowance depends on the route and ticket. Check your booking confirmation or call reservations before you travel.

What identification do I need?

Identification and travel-document requirements depend on your route. Confirm the current requirements with the relevant authorities and iFly before departure.

Which airport do iFly flights use?

The reference site lists iFly’s headquarters at Wilson Airport in Nairobi. Confirm the departure airport shown on your ticket before setting out.

Where are iFly sales offices?

The About page lists offices in Wajir, Eldoret, Lodwar, Malindi, Mombasa, Lamu and Eastleigh.
